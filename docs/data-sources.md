# Sourced menus, places and movements

Nearby menus use the [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) landmark coordinates and named branch groupings in `apps/api/modules/catalog/menu_locations.py`. The browser computes straight-line distance to each landmark and shows catalog locations within 5 km. The app sends the selected location ID as a menu filter. Device coordinates stay in the browser.

The local seed uses dated snapshots. HKUST venues come from the [Campus Services Office directory](https://cso.hkust.edu.hk/locations/restaurants). Off-campus locations are drawn from sources such as [THE LOHAS](https://www.thelohasmall.com/en/search-result?tag=Dining), [Park Central](https://www.park-central.com.hk/en/en-dining/), [East Point City](https://www.eastpointcity.com.hk/en/dining/) and [Fairwood branch listings](https://www.fairwood.com.hk/en/stores?tab=map). Individual rows link their listing. The café at Tai Po Tsai Village has a third-party address listing and no verified published menu. The village’s sourced food records come from 7-Eleven’s distribution lists for specific products or ranges.

The November 2026 menu snapshot records a selection of published dish and product names at campus venues and malls such as PopCorn 2, Park Central, MCP, Metro Town, TKO Plaza, TKO Spot, PopWalk, East Point City, The Lane and THE LOHAS. Every entry links the original menu or distribution list. A `branch` entry comes from a branch-specific source; a `brand` entry comes from a general Hong Kong brand menu or promotion. Prices, when shown, are reference prices printed in those sources. Older documents are marked as historical. The seed does not infer nutrition, allergens, stock, opening hours or today’s pricing. The separate fictional catalog still supplies the six meals for nutrition-based recommendations and demo logs.

Community’s **Local reviews** tab displays curated summaries of public Reddit discussions and OpenRice diner reviews, grouped by area. Each entry in `external_notes` identifies the platform, links the source, and records the date the link was checked. Examples include [HKUST canteen meals](https://www.reddit.com/r/HKUST/comments/1wn3wxi/hi_everyone/), [Hama Sushi at East Point City](https://www.openrice.com/zh/hongkong/review/--e6519426) and [Nabe Urawa at THE LOHAS](https://www.openrice.com/en/hongkong/review/--e6360204). The summaries describe individual experiences. Community member posts and credits use their own records.

Google Maps review content is not scraped or stored. The place card opens a Google Maps search so the visitor can read its live source. [Google's Places API policy](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) restricts caching and requires review-author and source attribution. A future live review integration must use an authorized API, follow those display rules and revisit retention terms. We did not import Xiaohongshu content without a reliable public source and reuse rights.

Exercise names, equipment and broad classifications were checked against the [ACE exercise library](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/) and linked ACE guides. The short movement cues are original summaries. The separate campus equipment list uses [HKUST DSTO](https://dst.hkust.edu.hk/support-%26-resources/sports-facilities/fitness-center) for LG4 and the Tsang Shiu Tim Sports Center together. The university lists treadmills, bike and rowing ergometers, elliptical trainers, free weights and multi-gym equipment without giving quantities or model numbers by room. The exercise detail names equipment needed for the movement; this is distinct from what the campus listing verifies. Workout planning uses the user’s declared equipment, stage and recent sessions.

To refresh it, update the checked listings in `apps/api/real_sources.py`, the menu snapshots in `apps/api/published_menus.py`, the review summaries in `apps/api/public_reviews.py`, and the equipment and movement sources in `apps/api/modules/workouts/sources.py`. Re-run the seed after migrations. Keep the checked date distinct from the date of a review or menu.

Image links in `apps/api/source_photos.py` match a menu and exact item or a movement page. Product images are from the named brand or distributor and may not represent how a particular branch serves a dish. Movement photos are from the cited exercise sources. The Workout view also links the official HKUST LG4 Fitness Center photo as a facility image. The app stores links and credits; it does not host copies or infer a substitute image for a dish.

## Photo recognition provider

The active app uses the configured [Nemotron 3.5 Super VL free model](https://openrouter.ai/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl%3Afree) for image input via [OpenRouter's multimodal chat format](https://openrouter.ai/blog/tutorials/send-image-to-llm/). The free endpoint's model page states that submitted content may be logged and should not contain confidential information or personal data. The UI therefore asks for food-only photos and re-encodes them in the browser to remove camera metadata. Model output is a tentative food-name list and rough edible-gram estimate where visually possible. Approximate nutrition is calculated separately from automatically matched USDA SR Legacy entries; the model never supplies nutrient totals. Users can correct the draft. This is a provider integration, not a food-image dataset.

The nutrition search uses the official [USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy download](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/) (April 2018) as a bundled compact index of 7,793 foods with complete energy/protein/carbohydrate/fat densities. `scripts/build_usda_index.py` regenerates `apps/api/data/usda_sr_legacy_2018.csv` from the official CSV archive. Searches use this local index first; the [FoodData Central API](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/) is a fallback for names absent from the index. The server matches each identified food against source descriptions and scales per-100-g densities by approximate edible grams. An optional editor lets users correct preparation, source, and quantity. USDA's public `DEMO_KEY` has a low quota, so fallback searches may be unavailable. US food descriptions may not represent a Hong Kong restaurant recipe; oil, sauce and hidden ingredients can be missed. If some foods have no reliable match or portion, the result shows and saves a clearly labelled **partial total for matched foods only**, lists excluded foods, and excludes that partial total from daily intake totals. Photo-only portion estimates are inherently rough; the stored item records whether grams came from a photo estimate, user note, or correction.
